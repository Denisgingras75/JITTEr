## Step 2: Create Your Account (5 min)

1. Go to https://login.gov and create an account (you need this for USPTO)
2. Go to https://patentcenter.uspto.gov
3. Log in with your login.gov account
4. You're in Patent Center

---

## Step 3: File It (10 min)

1. Click "New Submission"
2. Select "Provisional Application"
3. Fill in the Application Data Sheet:
   - Inventor: Denis Gingras
   - Full address on Martha's Vineyard
   - Correspondence address: same
   - Application type: Provisional
4. Upload your files:
   - Specification PDF → upload as "Specification"
   - Drawing JPEGs → upload as "Drawings"
   - Form SB/16 → upload as "Application Data Sheet" or "Cover Sheet"
   - Form SB/15a → upload as "Micro Entity Certification"
5. Select entity status: **Micro Entity**
6. Pay the fee: ~$65 (credit card works)
7. Review everything
8. Hit Submit

---

## Step 4: After You File (2 min)

1. **Save your filing receipt** — you'll get an application number. Screenshot it, email it to yourself, whatever
2. You can now legally say **"Patent Pending"** on everything Jitter-related
3. **Set a calendar reminder for 10 months from today** — that's when you need to start thinking about converting to a full utility patent (costs more, needs an attorney)
4. The provisional is never examined and never published — it just locks in your date
5. It expires in 12 months. If you don't file the utility patent by then, you lose the priority date (but you can still file, just without the early date)
