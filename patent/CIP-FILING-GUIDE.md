## Steps

### 1. Convert specification to PDF (~5 min)
- Open `CIP-PROVISIONAL-SPECIFICATION.md` in browser or markdown viewer
- Print → Save as PDF
- Name it: `CIP-PROVISIONAL-SPECIFICATION.pdf`

### 2. Draw the 5 figures (~20 min)
- Follow `CIP-DRAWING-INSTRUCTIONS.md`
- FIG 9-13, white paper, black pen
- Photo each, save as FIG-9.jpg through FIG-13.jpg

### 3. Fill cover sheet (~5 min)
- Download fresh SB/16: https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/sb0016.pdf
- Or reuse your filled one and change the title
- Check "Provisional" at top
- New title (above)
- Same inventor info:
  - Denis Michael Gingras
  - 22 Olive St, PO Box 1603
  - Oak Bluffs, MA 02557
  - denisgingras75@gmail.com

### 4. Micro entity form (~2 min)
- Same SB/15a as before
- You still qualify (< 4 applications, income under threshold)
- Refill or reuse

### 5. File on Patent Center (~10 min)
1. Go to https://patentcenter.uspto.gov
2. Log in (same login.gov account)
3. Click "New Submission"
4. Select "Provisional Application"
5. Upload:
   - Specification PDF → "Specification"
   - FIG JPEGs → "Drawings"
   - SB/16 → "Cover Sheet"
   - SB/15a → "Micro Entity Certification"
6. Entity status: Micro Entity
7. Pay $65
8. Submit

### 6. After filing (~2 min)
1. Save the filing receipt — you'll get a new application number
2. Screenshot it, email to yourself
3. Update FILING-RECEIPT.md with the new number
4. Both provisionals are now active

---

## Important notes

- This is filed as a SEPARATE provisional, not an amendment to #63/994,858
- The specification references the parent by number — that establishes the priority chain
- When you eventually file the utility patent, it will claim priority to BOTH provisionals
- You now have two clocks:
  - #63/994,858 expires March 2, 2027
  - This CIP provisional expires 12 months from its filing date
- The utility patent should claim priority to #63/994,858 (earlier date) for the original claims, and to this CIP for the new claims
