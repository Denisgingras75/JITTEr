## Defensive Value for WGH
- Reviews carry cryptographic proof of human authorship = non-replicable data quality moat
- "Patent pending" deters casual copycats
- Investor signaling: "we own the standard for verified human food reviews"
- Platform partnerships: WGH = proof-of-concept, others license

## EU AI Act (August 2, 2026)
- Article 50: AI-generated content must be labeled unless "human editorial process"
- Law doesn't specify HOW to prove human authorship — that's a standards gap
- Jitter's Smart Badge = technically defensible compliance artifact
- Risk: C2PA (backed by Google/Microsoft/Adobe) could become mandated standard instead
- Opportunity: C2PA covers device provenance, NOT author behavior — gap for Jitter

## "Know Your Human" Trend
- Real and accelerating (PYMNTS 2026, agentic commerce)
- KYC (exists) -> KYA (emerging) -> KYH (Jitter's territory)
- Global Passport = portable human identity signal across platforms
- Currently a positioning story, not yet a revenue story

## Bottom Line
The patent converts WGH's trust mechanism from a feature into defensible IP. That changes fundraising and acquisition conversations fundamentally. Best near-term move: land one EU compliance buyer before August 2026 as reference deal.
